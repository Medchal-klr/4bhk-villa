Privacy Policy

Owner Built Properties

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Owner Built Properties respects your privacy and is committed to handling information submitted through our property enquiry forms responsibly.

This Privacy Policy applies primarily to information submitted by people who enquire about properties advertised by Owner Built Properties, including enquiries submitted through Meta (Facebook or Instagram) lead forms.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a property enquiry, we may collect information such as:

  • Your full name
  • Your phone number
  • Information you provide in response to property-related questions
  • Other information you voluntarily provide when enquiring about the property

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide for purposes related to your property enquiry, including:

  • Sharing property information with you
  • Answering your questions about the property
  • Understanding your property requirements
  • Contacting you regarding your enquiry
  • Arranging a private property or site viewing when requested
  • Following up regarding your enquiry

3. Property Enquiries Through Meta

If you submit your information through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, the information you provide is made available to Owner Built Properties for follow-up regarding your enquiry.

Meta may separately process your information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

4. How We Handle Your Information

We use the information submitted through our enquiry forms only for legitimate purposes connected with responding to your property enquiry and providing related assistance.

We do not request sensitive personal information through our property enquiry form.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information when reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry or provide services related to your property enquiry.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Communications

By submitting your contact details through a property enquiry form, you understand that Owner Built Properties may contact you regarding the property enquiry, including to answer questions or arrange a site viewing if requested.

7. Your Choices

If you no longer wish to receive follow-up communications regarding your property enquiry, you may ask us to stop contacting you.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our property enquiry process or applicable requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how your information is handled in connection with a property enquiry, please contact Owner Built Properties through the contact options provided on our property website.
